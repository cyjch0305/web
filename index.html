Idea Presentation

01 — Title & Core Idea

Digital Echoes is an interactive generative art piece with "digital memory" as its core metaphor. Human interaction behaviors (mouse movement, clicks) serve as external inputs, while the AI Agent acts as the generating agent, receiving signals, triggering events, and leaving echo.The composition is made up of memory particles and elastic spheres: The collision of the spheres represents the encounter and impact of memories, while the trailing of particles and ripples represent the echoes that remain after the memory dissipates. Users are not mere viewers but co-creators. Every mouse movement and click by the user transmits signals to the AI agent, which calculates the movement, color, and trajectory of the particles in real time. The two collaborate to generate fleeting and irreproducible flowing digital images on the canvas.

02 — Motivation & Interests

I am interested in how memory is reshaped by digital systems and AI. When personal thoughts, actions and data are stored online, they become material that machines can reorganise, remix and reinterpret. Digital Echoes makes this process visible by translating interaction into dynamic visual traces. The project explores human-AI collaboration not as automation, but as a creative partnership.

Reflection

What worked well

The tab-structured website neatly presents the whole project. The browser physics simulation successfully embodies my core "echo" concept: user interactions spawn floating orbs, and collisions leave transient ripple marks as visual echoes of each moment of contact. The restrained colour palette establishes a quiet, meditative mood that aligns with the theme of fragile digital memory.

Limitations

The physics engine struggles when the quantity of orbs increases, causing frame-rate drops in some browsers. The visual system still lacks emergent complexity: ripple behaviour follows unchanging rules, and colour generation remains constrained within a predefined set of tones. The connection between audience input and AI-driven variation is subtle and not immediately perceptible to visitors.

Future Development

I plan to refine performance optimisations for the physics simulation. I want to introduce variable ripple lifespans controlled by AI logic, and build a lightweight memory archive that captures the visitor’s interaction history. I will also expand the colour system so AI can generate unique, cohesive colour variations rather than only drawing from fixed preset swatches.
